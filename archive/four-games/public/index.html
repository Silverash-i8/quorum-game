Game night

Four engineering business games. One laptop shows the big screen; everyone joins from a phone with the code or QR code.

On a phone? Enter the code from the big screen

Cell Out

Run a battery company for five years. Cheap cells and ethical shortcuts make money early. Reputation, sustainability and patents decide who wins.

3 or 4 companies · about 45 minutes

Host Cell Out

Player instructions · Datasheet

Chain Reaction

Four links of one supply chain. Nobody can see anyone else's stock, deliveries take two weeks, and the chain keeps or loses its contract together.

4 links, bots fill empty seats · about 30 minutes

Host Chain Reaction

Quorum

Four directors of one company. Each has a private report and a private bonus. Table what you know or keep quiet, then vote in secret.

4 directors, bots fill empty seats · about 40 minutes

Host Quorum

Patent Pending

Rival start-ups in a sealed-bid auction. Win patents, quota, suppliers and data, then license to each other, or copy and hope nobody sues.

3 or 4 companies · about 35 minutes

Host Patent Pending
